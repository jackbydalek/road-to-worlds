"Cute & Cozy UI SFX Pack" by Case Portman Audio

The sounds in this pack are named based on how they were originally designed, but that doesn't mean you have to use them that way!
Feel free to mix and match sounds, layer them together, randomize their pitch and/or volume, add your own effects, or use them in ways I haven't even thought of.

Get creative, experiment, and most importantly - have fun with them!


Included in this pack are: 

10x  Button Presses			-	General & neutral audio feedback for when a button or UI element is interacted with.
10x  Confirm
10x  Cancel
15x  Highlights 
10x  Click in				-	Great for controller buttons or mouse clicks, the initial click followed by a "Click out" can be really satisfying.
10x  Click out				-	See Above.
5x   Lock
5x   Unlock
10x  Currency				-	Great for general pickups too!
5x   Purchase
5x   Sell
17x  Dialogue Expressions	- 	Includes Happy, Sad, Angry and Neutral.
16x  Dialogue Blips			-	Scrolling text as letters and words appear.	
5x   Menu Open
5x   Menu Close
5x   Pause 
5x   UnPause 
8x   Transitions 			-	Good for moments when menus or screen appear with animations or wipes.
5x 	 Level Up
8x   Success 				-	Numbered sounds here resemble the failure sounds with the same number. (Use the same style)
8x   Failure				-	See above.
10x  Error
7x   Warning
7x   Clock Ticker
