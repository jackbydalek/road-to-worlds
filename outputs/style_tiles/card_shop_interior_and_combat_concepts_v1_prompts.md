# Card Shop Interior and Combat Concepts v1 — Generation Prompts

## Interior layout

Use case: infographic-diagram

Asset type: indoor environment layout and architectural concept sheet for the original low-poly card-shop game “Topdeck to Worlds”

Primary request: Visualize the interior of a bright contemporary local card shop as a practical low-poly game level. Create one polished landscape 16:9 concept sheet with three coordinated views: a clean top-down floor plan, an isometric cutaway of the entire shop, and a small eye-level interior vignette. Make the spatial relationships obvious and believable.

Shop layout: large glass storefront and centered entrance; new releases and sealed product immediately visible; glass singles counter and point-of-sale on the front-left with clear sightlines; colorful sleeves, deck boxes, binders and accessories on a clean product wall to the right; a central community trade/deck-tuning bar; four flexible play tables in the back half; one clearly distinguished feature table; event registration and pairings desk near the play area; staff storage/office behind the counter; wide accessible circulation loop; compact spectator edge that does not block shopping.

Event conversion: subtly show how the four casual tables align into two tournament rows while the feature table remains camera-ready. The store should work both as a daily retail shop and a tournament venue.

Style/medium: appealing original late-1990s-console-inspired low-poly 3D, broad planar geometry, selective rounded bevels, painted low-resolution textures, restrained cel shading, clean silhouettes, modern proportions and fixtures. Do not imitate any existing franchise or use copyrighted card-game branding.

Lighting/mood: bright daytime storefront light, white and pale cream walls, warm oak accents, matte teal counters, modern black track lights, soft cool window fill, warm practical lights, cheerful community atmosphere.

Materials: light terrazzo or sealed concrete floor, pale painted plaster, light oak, matte teal laminate, clear glass display cases, brushed metal, colorful matte plastic, felt playmats, paper posters, low-poly plants.

Color palette: parchment cream #F7E9C8, cocoa ink #241B17, walnut #57372E, muted teal #2D6F6A, with red-orange #E94E2F, leafy green #56A044, sky blue #36A3E6, lemon yellow #F4C542 and grape purple #9138A6 as retail and table accents.

Composition: large top-down plan on the left, large isometric cutaway in the center/right, small eye-level feature-table vignette and compact legend along the bottom. Use clear arrows for entry flow and event flow; use clean colored zone fills; retain generous spacing.

Text: “ENTRY”, “NEW RELEASES”, “SINGLES COUNTER”, “PRODUCT WALL”, “TRADE BAR”, “OPEN PLAY”, “FEATURE TABLE”, “EVENT DESK”, “STORAGE”, “DAILY FLOW”, “EVENT FLOW”.

Constraints: production-ready spatial reference, accurate furniture scale, clear circulation, readable labels, original store branding, modern real hobby-store cues, no dark rustic tavern, no bakery look, no fantasy shop, no photorealism, no neon sci-fi, no corporate showroom, no excessive clutter, no watermark, no dense paragraphs.

## Combat presentation

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: in-engine card-combat presentation concept for the original low-poly game “Topdeck to Worlds”

Primary request: Visualize what a full card battle should look like inside the bright contemporary low-poly card shop. Create one polished landscape 16:9 gameplay concept board. The main image is a dynamic three-quarter camera from just above the player’s seated shoulder, looking across a modern light-oak feature table with a teal felt playmat toward a friendly low-poly rival. The real shop remains clearly present around the match—glass storefront daylight, white walls, product shelves, track lights, event board, a few low-poly spectators, and colorful tournament tables—so combat feels like an exciting physical event in the overworld rather than a separate dark arena.

Table layout: physical mirrored play space with three Prep slots behind three Plated slots for each side; player's Prep closest to the player and Plated toward center, opponent mirrored; tactile deck and discard piles on each player’s right; a chunky physical Chef/life token on each left; cards slightly oversized for readability but still believable as physical cards; open central combat lane; subtle printed zone graphics woven into the felt mat.

Card treatment: cards retain the cozy illustrated-paper identity—cream stock, dark ink outlines, colorful food-creature art, tactile sleeve edges—and are noticeably more detailed than the low-poly environment. Selected card lifts 2–3 cm, tilts toward camera, and gains a warm soft rim; legal targets gain simple illuminated bases; hovered card expands as one crisp readable focus card near the lower edge without covering the board.

Action language: an attacking card slides forward and tilts, with a brief paper-cutout swoosh made from chili, leaf, candy, bowl or pickle motifs; damage appears as chunky stamped number tokens; healing uses small floating paper hearts; no explosive realistic magic. Keep effects readable, playful and materially connected to printed cards.

HUD: minimal and spatially anchored. Use a small clean turn indicator on the overhead event display, a physical illuminated END TURN button or tabletop device at the player’s right, compact Chef life numbers beside the physical tokens, and a narrow bottom instruction strip only during selections. Avoid a permanent full-screen dashboard.

Composition: main gameplay view occupies about 70 percent. Along one edge include three compact callout diagrams showing (1) base table layout, (2) card focus/target states, and (3) attack animation language. Use short labels and arrows only.

Style/medium: original modern low-poly 3D, broad planar forms, selective bevels, hand-painted low-resolution textures, restrained cel shading, bright high-key lighting, clean silhouettes, friendly expressive characters. The cards are crisp illustrated 2D surfaces within the low-poly world.

Color palette: light cream, light oak, muted teal, clear sky blue and warm honey light, with red-orange, leafy green, sky blue, lemon yellow and grape purple affinity accents.

Text: “PREP”, “PLATED”, “CHEF”, “DECK”, “DISCARD”, “TURN”, “END TURN”, “CARD FOCUS”, “LEGAL TARGET”, “ATTACK”.

Constraints: immediately readable as a real card-game match in a modern hobby store; practical Godot presentation reference; original cards and characters only; show both physicality and game clarity. Avoid dark void backgrounds, holographic sci-fi tables, giant opaque HUD panels, tiny unreadable cards, photorealism, realistic fire or explosions, copyrighted trading-card frames or logos, extra text, and watermarks.
