# Topdeck Style Tile v1 — Generation Prompt

Use case: infographic-diagram

Asset type: production game visual-identity style tile for the Godot card game “Topdeck to Worlds”

Primary request: Create one polished, cohesive, landscape 16:9 brand style tile for a playful collectible card game about hand-drawn food-animal creatures, neighborhood card-shop culture, Topdeck matches, and progressing from weekly locals to Worlds.

Scene/backdrop: warm cream recycled paper board laid over a walnut tabletop, with a tidy editorial grid and small pieces of tape, ink stamps, recipe-card dividers, card sleeves, and subtle crumbs; sophisticated and presentation-ready, not cluttered.

Style/medium: charming hand-inked illustration, chunky imperfect dark-brown outlines, flat screen-printed color, tactile paper grain, slightly misregistered print edges, playful indie tabletop game art; warm, confident, memorable; retain excellent UI readability.

Composition/framing: one complete style tile with clearly separated modules: (1) expressive hand-lettered wordmark, (2) typography pairing, (3) core neutral palette and five affinity swatches, (4) five matching custom affinity icons, (5) UI component samples including paper tab, primary button, selected button, badge and tooltip, (6) one sample creature card treatment with a cute original food-animal doodle, (7) a small warm card-shop/living-table mood vignette with felt playmat and soft light, (8) texture and shape-language samples. Strong hierarchy and generous spacing.

Color palette: parchment cream #F7E9C8, dark cocoa ink #241B17, walnut #57372E, muted teal #2D6F6A; affinity colors are Spicy red-orange #E94E2F, Hearty leafy green #56A044, Sweet sky blue #36A3E6, Fresh lemon yellow #F4C542, Funky grape purple #9138A6. Affinity color is semantic accent only; foundations remain warm and tactile.

Typography: condensed hand-painted display lettering paired with an extremely legible friendly sans-serif for rules and body copy.

Text (verbatim, use only these short labels): “Topdeck”, “Topdeck to Worlds”, “DISPLAY / HEADLINES”, “RULES / UI”, “SPICY”, “HEARTY”, “SWEET”, “FRESH”, “FUNKY”, “PLAY”, “DRAFT”, “WEEKLY LOCALS”.

Icon concepts: Spicy is a chili flame, Hearty is a steaming bowl, Sweet is a wrapped candy, Fresh is a lime leaf, Funky is a pickle with sparkle; all redrawn in the same chunky hand-inked line style, not emoji.

UI shape language: 2–3 px dark ink outlines, 10 px soft-rounded corners, offset paper shadows, slightly irregular edges, cream paper fills, stamped selected states, tactile hover/press cues.

Constraints: visually cohesive and immediately useful as a real art-direction reference; high contrast; custom original artwork; all modules legible at a glance; no existing game or trading-card intellectual property; no photorealism; no glossy corporate UI; no neon sci-fi; no black dashboard panels; no operating-system emoji; no watermark; no tiny paragraphs; no extra branding or slogans.
